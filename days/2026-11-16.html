Question 1

Maths, Parabola JEE Main 2025 (April session)

Let $P$ be the parabola whose focus is $(-2,1)$ and whose directrix is $2x+y+2=0$. Then the sum of the ordinates of the points on $P$ whose abscissa is $-2$ is

  1. A$\dfrac{3}{2}$
  2. B$\dfrac{5}{2}$
  3. C$\dfrac{1}{4}$
  4. D$\dfrac{3}{4}$
Show solutionHide solution

Answer: (A) $\dfrac{3}{2}$

Every point on a parabola is the same distance from the focus as from the directrix. Use this rule to find the parabola's equation, then put in $x=-2$.

  1. Let $(x,y)$ be a point on $P$. Its distance from the focus $(-2,1)$ is:

    $$\sqrt{(x+2)^2+(y-1)^2}$$

  2. Its distance from the line $2x+y+2=0$ is:

    $$\dfrac{|2x+y+2|}{\sqrt{5}}$$

  3. Set the two distances equal and square both sides. This gives the equation of the parabola:

    $$\begin{aligned} &(x+2)^2+(y-1)^2 \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{(2x+y+2)^2}{5} \end{aligned}$$

  4. "Abscissa $-2$" means $x=-2$. Put this in. The term $(x+2)^2$ becomes $0$, and $2x+y+2$ becomes $y-2$:

    $$(y-1)^2=\dfrac{(y-2)^2}{5}$$

  5. Multiply both sides by $5$ and expand:

    $$\begin{aligned} &5y^2-10y+5 \\[6pt] &= y^2-4y+4 \end{aligned}$$

  6. Move everything to one side to get a quadratic in $y$:

    $$4y^2-6y+1=0$$

    Its discriminant is $36-16=20>0$, so there are two real points.

  7. The "ordinates" are the $y$-values of these points. For a quadratic $ay^2+by+c=0$, the sum of the roots is $-\dfrac{b}{a}$:

    $$\begin{aligned} &y_1+y_2 \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{6}{4} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{3}{2} \end{aligned}$$

Question 2

Maths, Parabola JEE Main 2025 (April session)

The axis of a parabola is the line $y=x$. Its vertex and focus are in the first quadrant, at distances $\sqrt{2}$ and $2\sqrt{2}$ units from the origin, respectively. If the point $(1, k)$ lies on the parabola, then a possible value of $k$ is:

  1. A$4$
  2. B$9$
  3. C$3$
  4. D$8$
Show solutionHide solution

Answer: (B) $9$

Find the focus and the directrix. Then use the fact that every point on a parabola is the same distance from the focus as from the directrix.

  1. A point on $y=x$ in the first quadrant has the form $(t,t)$ with $t>0$. Its distance from the origin is $\sqrt{2}\,t$. So the vertex is $(1,1)$ and the focus is $(2,2)$.

  2. The vertex lies halfway between the focus and the directrix. The directrix is perpendicular to the axis $y=x$, and the point opposite the focus $(2,2)$ across the vertex $(1,1)$ is $(0,0)$. So the directrix is the line through the origin perpendicular to $y=x$:

    $$x+y=0$$

  3. For a point $(x,y)$ on the parabola, the squared distance to the focus equals the squared distance to the directrix:

    $$\begin{aligned} &(x-2)^2+(y-2)^2 \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{(x+y)^2}{2} \end{aligned}$$

  4. Multiply both sides by 2 and simplify:

    $$\begin{aligned} x^2+y^2-2xy &= 8x+8y-16 \\[6pt] (x-y)^2 &= 8(x+y)-16 \end{aligned}$$

  5. Put $x=1$ and $y=k$:

    $$\begin{aligned} (1-k)^2 &= 8(1+k)-16 \\[6pt] k^2-10k+9 &= 0 \end{aligned}$$

  6. Factorise to solve:

    $$(k-1)(k-9)=0$$

    So $k=1$ or $k=9$. Only $9$ appears in the options.
