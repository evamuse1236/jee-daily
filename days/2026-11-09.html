Question 1

Physics, Laws of motion JEE Main 2025 (January session)

A massless spring gets elongated by amount $x_1$ under a tension of $5\ \text{N}$. Its elongation is $x_2$ under a tension of $7\ \text{N}$. For an elongation of $\left(5x_1 - 2x_2\right)$, what will the tension in the spring be?

  1. A$39\ \text{N}$
  2. B$15\ \text{N}$
  3. C$11\ \text{N}$
  4. D$20\ \text{N}$
Show solutionHide solution

Answer: (C) $11\ \text{N}$

Use Hooke's law, which says the tension in a spring equals its stiffness times its stretch: $T = kx$.

  1. Write Hooke's law for the first case, where the tension is $5\ \text{N}$.

    $$kx_1 = 5$$

  2. Write it for the second case, where the tension is $7\ \text{N}$.

    $$kx_2 = 7$$

  3. For the new stretch $(5x_1 - 2x_2)$, the tension is $k$ times that stretch.

    $$\begin{aligned} T &= k(5x_1 - 2x_2) \\[6pt] &= 5(kx_1) - 2(kx_2) \end{aligned}$$

  4. Put in the values from Steps 1 and 2.

    $$\begin{aligned} T &= 5(5) - 2(7) \\[6pt] &= 25 - 14 \\[6pt] &= 11\ \text{N} \end{aligned}$$

Question 2

Maths, Quadratic equation JEE Main 2025 (January session)

If the set of all $a \in \mathbb{R}$ for which the equation $2x^2+(a-5)x+15=3a$ has no real root is the interval $(\alpha, \beta)$, and $X=\{x \in \mathbb{Z} : \alpha < x < \beta\}$, then $\displaystyle\sum_{x \in X} x^2$ is equal to:

  1. A2109
  2. B2129
  3. C2119
  4. D2139
Show solutionHide solution

Answer: (D) 2139

A quadratic has no real roots exactly when its discriminant is negative. This condition gives the interval for $a$. Then we add the squares of the integers inside that interval.

  1. Move every term to one side so the equation is in standard form.

    $$2x^2+(a-5)x+(15-3a)=0$$

  2. Write the discriminant $D=b^2-4ac$.

    $$\begin{aligned} D &= (a-5)^2-8(15-3a) \\[6pt] &= a^2-10a+25-120+24a \\[6pt] &= a^2+14a-95 \end{aligned}$$

  3. For no real root we need $D<0$. Find where $a^2+14a-95=0$.

    $$\begin{aligned} a &= -7 \pm \sqrt{49+95} \\[6pt] &= -7 \pm 12 \end{aligned}$$

    So $a=-19$ or $a=5$.

  4. The expression $a^2+14a-95$ is negative between its two roots. So $D<0$ when $-19<a<5$, which gives $\alpha=-19$ and $\beta=5$.

  5. The integers strictly between $-19$ and $5$ are $-18, -17, \dots, 4$. A negative number has the same square as its positive partner, so the sum of the squares is

    $$(1^2+\dots+18^2)+0^2+(1^2+\dots+4^2)$$

  6. Use the formula $1^2+2^2+\dots+n^2 = \dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$.

    $$\begin{aligned} \sum_{1}^{18} k^2 &= \dfrac{18\cdot19\cdot37}{6} \\[6pt] &= 2109 \end{aligned}$$
    $$1^2+2^2+3^2+4^2=30$$

  7. Add the two parts.

    $$2109+30=2139$$
