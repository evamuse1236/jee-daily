Question 1

Physics, Laws of motion JEE Main 2024 (April session)

A wooden block of mass $5\ \mathrm{kg}$ rests on a soft horizontal floor. When an iron cylinder of mass $25\ \mathrm{kg}$ is placed on top of the block, the floor gives way. The block and the cylinder then move down together with an acceleration of $0.1\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. The action force of the system on the floor is equal to:

  1. A$196\ \mathrm{N}$
  2. B$291\ \mathrm{N}$
  3. C$294\ \mathrm{N}$
  4. D$297\ \mathrm{N}$
Show solutionHide solution

Apply Newton's second law to the block and cylinder together. Then use Newton's third law: the force the system pushes on the floor equals the force the floor pushes back on the system.

  1. The block and the cylinder move together, so treat them as one body. Its total mass is:

    $$m = 5 + 25 = 30\ \mathrm{kg}$$

  2. Two forces act on the system. Its weight $mg$ acts downwards, and the floor's normal force $N$ acts upwards. Take $g = 9.8\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$.

    $$mg = 30 \times 9.8 = 294\ \mathrm{N}$$

  3. The system speeds up as it moves downwards, so the downward force must be larger than the upward force. Newton's second law gives:

    $$mg - N = ma$$

  4. Rearrange to find $N$:

    $$N = m(g - a) = 30 \times (9.8 - 0.1) = 30 \times 9.7 = 291\ \mathrm{N}$$

  5. By Newton's third law, the system pushes on the floor with the same size of force, $291\ \mathrm{N}$, directed downwards.

Answer: (B) $291\ \mathrm{N}$

Question 2

Maths, Quadratic equation JEE Main 2019 (January session)

If one real root of the quadratic equation $81x^{2}+kx+256=0$ is the cube of the other root, then a value of $k$ is:

  1. A$-81$
  2. B$100$
  3. C$144$
  4. D$-300$
Show solutionHide solution

Call the roots $\alpha$ and $\alpha^3$. The product of roots gives $\alpha$, and the sum of roots then gives $k$.

  1. For $ax^2+bx+c=0$, the product of the roots is $\dfrac{c}{a}$. Here that is:

    $$\alpha \cdot \alpha^3 = \alpha^4 = \frac{256}{81}$$

  2. Take the fourth root. Since $\alpha$ is real, there are two possible values:

    $$\alpha = \pm\frac{4}{3}$$

  3. The sum of the roots is $-\dfrac{b}{a}$, so:

    $$\alpha + \alpha^3 = -\frac{k}{81}$$

  4. Take $\alpha = \frac{4}{3}$. Then $\alpha^3 = \frac{64}{27}$, and the sum is:

    $$\frac{4}{3} + \frac{64}{27} = \frac{36}{27} + \frac{64}{27} = \frac{100}{27}$$

  5. Set this equal to $-\frac{k}{81}$ and solve for $k$:

    $$-\frac{k}{81} = \frac{100}{27} \;\Rightarrow\; k = -\frac{100 \times 81}{27} = -300$$

  6. Taking $\alpha = -\frac{4}{3}$ instead gives $k = 300$. That value is not among the options, so the answer is $k = -300$.

Answer: (D) $-300$
