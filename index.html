Question 1

Physics, Laws of motion JEE Main 2024 (April session)

A wooden block of mass $5\ \text{kg}$ rests on a soft horizontal floor. When an iron cylinder of mass $25\ \text{kg}$ is placed on top of the block, the floor yields, and the block and the cylinder together go down with an acceleration of $0.1\ \text{m s}^{-2}$. The action force of the system on the floor is equal to:

  1. A$196\ \text{N}$
  2. B$291\ \text{N}$
  3. C$294\ \text{N}$
  4. D$297\ \text{N}$
Show solutionHide solution

Answer: (B) $291\ \text{N}$

Apply Newton's second law to the block and cylinder taken together. By Newton's third law, the force the system puts on the floor equals the floor's upward push on the system.

  1. Find the total mass of the system.

    $$M = 5 + 25 = 30\ \text{kg}$$

  2. Two forces act on the system: its weight $Mg$ pulls down and the floor's normal force $N$ pushes up. The system speeds up downwards, so the weight is larger than $N$.

    $$Mg - N = Ma$$

  3. Rearrange to get $N$, using $g = 9.8\ \text{m s}^{-2}$ and $a = 0.1\ \text{m s}^{-2}$.

    $$\begin{aligned} N &= M(g - a) \\ &= 30(9.8 - 0.1) \\ &= 30 \times 9.7 \\ &= 291\ \text{N} \end{aligned}$$

  4. By Newton's third law, the action force of the system on the floor has the same size as $N$.

    $$F = 291\ \text{N}$$

Question 2

Maths, Quadratic equation JEE Main 2019 (January session)

If one real root of the quadratic equation $81x^{2}+kx+256=0$ is the cube of the other root, then a value of $k$ is:

  1. A$-81$
  2. B$100$
  3. C$144$
  4. D$-300$
Show solutionHide solution

Answer: (D) $-300$

Call the roots $\alpha$ and $\alpha^3$. The product of roots gives $\alpha$, and the sum of roots then gives $k$.

  1. For $ax^2+bx+c=0$, the product of the roots is $\dfrac{c}{a}$.

    $$\alpha \cdot \alpha^3 = \dfrac{256}{81}$$

  2. This means $\alpha^4 = \dfrac{256}{81} = \left(\dfrac{4}{3}\right)^4$. Since $\alpha$ is real, $\alpha = \pm\dfrac{4}{3}$.

  3. The sum of the roots is $-\dfrac{b}{a}$.

    $$\alpha + \alpha^3 = -\dfrac{k}{81}$$

  4. Take $\alpha = \dfrac{4}{3}$:

    $$\begin{aligned} \alpha+\alpha^3 &= \dfrac{4}{3}+\dfrac{64}{27} \\ &= \dfrac{36}{27}+\dfrac{64}{27} \\ &= \dfrac{100}{27} \end{aligned}$$

  5. Put this into the sum equation and solve for $k$:

    $$\begin{aligned} -\dfrac{k}{81} &= \dfrac{100}{27} \\ k &= -300 \end{aligned}$$

  6. If $\alpha = -\dfrac{4}{3}$ instead, the sum changes sign and $k = 300$. That is not an option, so the answer is $k=-300$.
